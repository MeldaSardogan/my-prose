# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the My Prose project.

Participation is welcome regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, education, or socioeconomic status.

We expect all participants to communicate respectfully and contribute to a constructive community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Treating other people with respect, patience, and empathy.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences, and technical approaches.
- Giving constructive and actionable feedback.
- Accepting constructive criticism professionally.
- Focusing discussions on what is best for the project and its community.
- Acknowledging the work and contributions of others.
- Asking questions when something is unclear.
- Helping new contributors understand the project and its processes.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and making an effort to correct them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or threats.
- Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive behavior, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring reasonable requests from maintainers.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, secrets, credentials, or harmful content.
- Disrupting discussions, reviews, issues, or pull requests.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern.
- Any behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Constructive Technical Discussions

Technical disagreements are normal and can improve the project.

When discussing different solutions:

- Criticize the code or proposal, not the person.
- Explain the technical reason for your feedback.
- Provide examples or alternatives where possible.
- Avoid sarcasm, insults, and unnecessarily aggressive language.
- Assume good intentions unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Accept that maintainers may choose a different solution after considering the available options.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues.
- Pull requests.
- Code reviews.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Project-related email communication.
- Community chats and meetings.
- Events where a person is representing the project.

It also applies when someone is publicly representing the project or acting in an official project role.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying acceptable and unacceptable behavior.
- Responding to reports fairly and consistently.
- Removing, editing, or rejecting contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
- Applying temporary or permanent restrictions when necessary.
- Protecting the privacy and safety of people who report incidents.
- Avoiding conflicts of interest when reviewing a report.

Maintainers must not use their position to harass, intimidate, or unfairly exclude contributors.

## Reporting a Problem

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately.

Send your report to:

`YOUR_CODE_OF_CONDUCT_EMAIL`

Do not report sensitive conduct issues in a public GitHub issue, pull request, or discussion.

A useful report should include:

- A description of what happened.
- Where and when the incident occurred.
- Links, screenshots, or other relevant evidence.
- The names or usernames of the people involved.
- Whether the behavior is continuing.
- Any immediate safety concerns.

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Response Process

After receiving a report, maintainers will aim to:

1. Confirm receipt of the report within seven days.
2. Review the available information.
3. Contact involved parties when appropriate.
4. Determine whether this Code of Conduct was violated.
5. Take proportionate corrective action.
6. Inform the reporter when the review is complete, where possible.

The project cannot guarantee a specific outcome, but every good-faith report will be taken seriously.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on the severity and history of the behavior, possible actions include:

### 1. Informal Correction

A private explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate and a request to stop.

### 2. Formal Warning

A written warning describing the violation and the expected change in behavior.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

Temporary restriction from project interaction, such as commenting, reviewing, or submitting contributions.

### 4. Removal of Content

Removal or rejection of comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate project standards.

### 5. Permanent Ban

Permanent removal from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

## Appeals

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration by contacting:

`YOUR_CODE_OF_CONDUCT_EMAIL`

## No Retaliation

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is prohibited.

Good-faith reports will not result in punishment, even when an investigation does not confirm a violation.
